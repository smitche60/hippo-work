# Research queue

Say "research X" or "deep research X" and it lands here. The morning brief run executes
what's due and puts a Research section in the brief; findings files go to reports/.
One-shot items run once and move to Done below. Watches repeat until removed. `#W` ids
are their own namespace, separate from the board's `[#N]` task ids.

High-signal contract: named sources only; anything uncertain is left out or explicitly
marked uncertain; a watch stays silent unless it actually found something. The morning
brief runs at most one deep-research item per morning.
