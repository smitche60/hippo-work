# Research queue

Say "research X" or "deep research X" and it lands here. Say "run research" and the
assistant executes what's due (AGENTS.md, "Research"); findings files go to reports/. The
morning brief says how many items are due. One-shot items run once and move to Done below.
Watches repeat until removed. `#W` ids are their own namespace, separate from the board's
`[#N]` task ids.

High-signal contract: named sources only; anything uncertain is left out or explicitly
marked uncertain; a watch stays silent unless it actually found something. One run
executes at most one deep-research item.
